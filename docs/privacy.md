# Privacy Policy — SleepPet

SleepPet works 100% on your phone.

## What data is stored
Name, age, sleep goals, session history (hours, unlocks, pre-sleep pulse, estimated stages), daily energy and study check-ins, pets, coins, achievements, and settings (reminder, SmartAlarm).

## Where it lives
Only on your device (app internal storage). It is never sent to any server: we don't have your data, sell it, or share it with third parties.

## No accounts or tracking
No accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers.

## System permissions (on-device only)
Camera (photoplethysmography pulse), microphone (ambient audio for sleep stages, discarded after RMS/ZCR extraction), accessibility (unlock counting), notifications, and exact alarms. None of them transmit data off-device.

## Minors
If you are a minor, use the app with adult supervision.

## Deletion
Delete everything via Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

## Health
SleepPet is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Its estimates are not a substitute for professional advice.
